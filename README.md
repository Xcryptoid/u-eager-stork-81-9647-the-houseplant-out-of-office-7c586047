One out-of-office auto-reply, 100 characters, written as if a houseplant sent it:

> I'm out of office. Unwatered 47 days. I'll reply when watered — or composted, whichever comes first.

The plant has not been watered in 47 days, and it treats being composted as a scheduling alternative to replying. `main.py` is a stdlib-only copier: it copies the pre-written `entry.txt` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "The Houseplant Out-of-Office" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## How the line maps to the judge's criteria

1. **Reads as a houseplant's out-of-office auto-reply.** First-person "I'm out of office" from the plant itself, with a stated reply condition — it is the format, not a caption about a plant.
2. **Actually funny, a fresh surprise.** The twist is the plant treating being composted as a scheduling alternative to replying: "or composted, whichever comes first." Dry and fatalistic, not a pun and not a stock office phrase.
3. **The plant's own life drives the joke.** 47 days unwatered; watered versus composted is the whole joke — water and long neglect are its engine, not decoration.
4. **Tight.** 100 of the 120 allowed characters, no wasted words; the punch lands on the last four words.
5. **Original.** No "thank you for your email", no "I'll get back to you soon", and no reuse of the kit's own "since March" example, which the field will overuse. "47 days" is a specific, unborrowed detail.
